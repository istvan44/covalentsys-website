Build.

Custom AI tools built on your documents, your workflows, your data. Implementation that actually ships, not a slide deck.

When the Assess engagement surfaces something worth building, I build it. Retrieval systems, workflow automation, custom agents, integrations. Scope depends on what we're building.

  • Customer support and ticket deflection. Answer common questions instantly, escalate the rest with full context attached.
  • Internal knowledge bases. Find that contract, SOP, or slide deck buried in five years of Google Drive.
  • Company handbooks and HR. Benefits, policies, and procedures answered without interrupting HR every time.
  • Standardized team AI workflows. Stop every employee inventing their own AI approach. Standardized tools, prompts, and guardrails with audit trails built in. Critical in regulated industries.
